Submission Guidelines

We're so glad you want to share your story. Before you send us your writing, please read these simple guidelines.

Please make sure:

  • You are the original author. The story is yours and has not been copied from someone else.
  • You are 16 or older. If you are under 16, please have a parent or guardian contact us first.
  • By sending us your story, you are giving us permission to publish it on our website. If your story is selected, we will contact you before publishing.

We strongly encourage you to:

  • Use a pseudonym (a fake name) to protect your identity.
  • Avoid sharing identifiable information. Please don't include your full real name, address, school, workplace, or social media handles.
  • Include a content warning if needed. If your story mentions anything potentially triggering (e.g., homophobia, violence, discrimination), please add a brief note at the top, like: "CW: mentions of bullying."

Your submission should be between 50 and 650 words.

What to include in your email:

  • Subject Line: The category your story fits into (e.g., "Coming Out Story," "First Love," "Advice," "Ally Experience," "Self-Discovery")
  • Title: Give your piece a title.
  • Your Story: You can attach a Word document or paste your story directly in the email.

Your email will never be shared.

Ready to submit? Send your story to: qtopia.submissions@gmail.com
